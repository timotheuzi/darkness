Overview

Darkness BBS is a multi-user, terminal-style cyberpunk RPG inspired by classic BBS games like MajorMUD. Players explore a procedurally generated grid, engage in tactical combat, customize their builds via manual stat training, and compete with other users in a gritty, neon-soaked world.

Installation

Prerequisites

  • Python 3.11+
  • pip
  • make (optional)

Local Setup

# Clone and enter directory
git clone https://github.com/timotheuzi/darknesses.git
cd darknesses

# Create environment
make venv
source .venv/bin/activate

# Install dependencies
make setup

# Nuke database and build fresh schema
make repair

# Generate world data
make init

# Start the grid
make run

Getting Started

  1. Navigate to the terminal in your browser.
  2. Click "Initialize New Profile" to create a character.
  3. Enter a handle (username) and password.
  4. Select your Race (Augmentation Path) and Class (Operational Class).
  5. Optionally customize your 6 base stats (STR, INT, WIL, AGI, HEA, CHA) — total must not exceed 85 points.
  6. Log in and arrive at The Neon Hub, the grid's central safe zone.

Stats Reference

STR (Strength): Physical power. Increases Attack and carrying capacity. Essential for melee classes.
AGI (Agility): Speed and reflexes. Increases Defense, crit chance, and stealth effectiveness. Key for dodging and Thief/Ninja.
HEA (Health): Physical resilience. Increases max HP. Vital for survivability.
INT (Intelligence): Mental acuity. Powers tech, netrunning, and spellcasting. Increases mana for some classes.
WIL (Willpower): Mental fortitude. Increases max Mana and resistance to mind effects. Core for Priests.
CHA (Charisma): Social presence. Used by Fixers and Tricksters. Affects NPC interactions and some abilities.

Class Strategies

Street Samurai: Physical combat specialist. High attack, excels in direct confrontations.
Netrunner: Tech-hacker. Uses water/ice and air-based attacks. High mana pool, debuffs enemies.
Techie: Drone operator. Earth and fire damage, can heal with nanobots.
Medie: Field medic. Healing and support abilities, can deal water/rot damage.
Fixer: Social manipulator. Drains credits, calls in attacks.
Thief: Stealth assassin. Backstab crits, poison, smoke bombs. Can STEAL while hidden.
Heavy: Tank specialist. High HP and defense, earth-based AOE attacks.
Psycher: Psychic warrior. Mind-based attacks, telekinesis, shields.
Warlock: Dark spellcaster. Curses, chaos damage, blood pacts.
Priest: Holy healer. Healing, blessings, divine shields.
Trickster: Chaos agent. Random effects, stealth, can STEAL while hidden.
Jade Dragon: Martial artist. Chi-based attacks, unarmed combat bonus.
Ninja: Shadow warrior. Stealth, teleport, kunai throws, high crit from concealment.

Game Commands

Navigation

  • N, S, E, W - Movement
  • EXIT - Log out safely

Information

  • L / LOOK - Scan sector. LOOK to examine entity.
  • WHO - List active nodes
  • TOP - Top 25 ranking
  • WALL - Top 25 deaths
  • ST / STATUS - Detailed profile
  • I / INVENTORY - List gear
  • HELP / ? - Command manual

Communication

  • SAY - Local chat
  • BROADCAST - Global chat

Combat & Action

  • A - Attack one round
  • AA - Auto-attack until victory/defeat
  • - Use class skill (e.g., HCK, BLD, PTC)
  • STEALTH / SNEAK - Hide (influenced by AGI and Class)
  • STEAL - Steal credits (Thief/Trickster only, must be hidden)
  • REST - Recover HP/Mana (requires standing still)

Items

  • USE - Consumables/Drugs
  • GET / DROP - Ground interaction
  • EQUIP - Manage hardware
  • LIST / BUY / SELL - Commerce

Combat System

Damage & Crits

Damage: max(1, ATK - DEF/2) ± variance. Critical Hits: 2.0x damage. Chance based on AGI (physical) or INT (abilities).

Elemental Wheel

Fire > Air > Earth > Water > Fire

Strength: 1.5x damage. Weakness: 0.5x damage.

Weapon Speed

10 Speed Bonus = 1 extra attack/round. 10 Agility = 1 extra attack/round.

World Structure

The grid consists of 8 distinct zones:

  • The Neon Hub - Central safe zone, starting location
  • Slums - Low-level area with basic threats
  • Industrial - Mid-level area with corporate presence
  • Corporate - High-level area with elite enemies
  • Undergrid - Dark area with unique loot
  • Neon - Vibrant area with balanced challenges
  • Wastes - Dangerous area with rare rewards
  • Nexus - High-level area with powerful foes
  • Undercity - End-game area with legendary gear

Items & Equipment

Items come in several types:

  • Weapons - Increase attack power and may have speed bonuses
  • Armor - Increase defense and may have stat bonuses
  • Consumables - Restore HP or provide temporary effects
  • Drugs - Provide stat boosts but risk addiction
  • Scrolls - Teleport to specific locations

Character Progression

Level up by gaining experience from defeating NPCs and players. Each level grants:

  • +1 Stat Point (use TRAIN to spend)
  • +20 Max HP
  • +10 Max Mana
  • New class abilities at levels 1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30

Karma & Reputation

Your karma affects NPC behavior and available quests:

  • Saint (80+) - Holy alignment, good NPCs are friendly
  • Paragon (50-79) - Lawful alignment
  • Lawful (20-49) - Neutral-good alignment
  • Neutral (-19 to 19) - Balanced alignment
  • Renegade (-49 to -20) - Neutral-evil alignment
  • Outlaw (-79 to -50) - Chaotic alignment
  • Utter Villain (-100 to -80) - Evil alignment, aggressive NPCs target you

Stealth System

Use STEALTH or SNEAK to become hidden. While hidden:

  • You can perform backstab attacks for bonus damage
  • You avoid auto-attack from NPCs
  • Moving may reveal you based on threat level
  • Thief and Trickster classes have better stealth chances

Drug & Addiction System

Drugs provide powerful temporary boosts but carry risks:

  • Each use has a chance to increase addiction
  • Higher addiction causes withdrawal damage over time
  • Detox abilities and time can reduce addiction
  • Dealers in the undercity buy drugs for higher prices

NPC Aggression & Stalking

NPCs may become aggressive based on karma alignment. Defeated NPCs can stalk you:

  • Stalking NPCs follow you to new rooms
  • Each move reduces stalk count by 1
  • Stalked NPCs will re-engage in combat

Party System

Team up with other players to explore and move together.

  • PARTY CREATE - Start a new party as leader
  • PARTY INVITE - Invite a player in your current room
  • PARTY ACCEPT - Join a party after receiving an invite
  • PARTY LEAVE - Exit your current party
  • PARTY STATUS - See current members
  • Group Movement: When the leader moves, all online party members move with them.

Server Administration

# Full database reset (wipes players)
make repair

# Wipes world data but keeps players
make clean

# Generates new world state
make init

# Starts the AI daemon
python manage.py process_bots
